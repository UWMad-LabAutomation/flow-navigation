CURRENT SIMULATION AND CAMERA SCANNER

ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Harmonic, Nav2, and RViz run together in WSL2. A generated
maze supplies matching Gazebo walls and an occupancy-grid map. AMCL estimates
the robot's pose, SmacPlanner2D plans routes, and Regulated Pure Pursuit drives
the simulated FLOW base. RViz displays the map, costmaps, scan, and planned path.
This remains a simulation; real-base integration is future work.

A simulated RealSense D435i depth camera now supplies /scan in place of the
360-degree lidar. The sensing pipeline is:

  D435i depth camera -> /camera/depth/points (PointCloud2)
  -> pointcloud_to_laserscan -> /scan (LaserScan)

The point cloud is transformed and height-filtered to remove floor returns
before projection into /scan. AMCL, the obstacle costmaps, and collision monitor
use this camera-derived scan. Camera mounting height and horizontal field of
view (scan width) are adjustable. Current defaults are a provisional 1.70 m
height and approximately 90 degrees of forward view, tilted down to see the
ground about 1 m beyond the front edge of the base.

INTENDED PATH-FOLLOWING BEHAVIOR

The robot travels with its short side facing forward, using forward motion
and yaw at up to 0.35 m/s, with no sideways motion during normal path following.
It should drive steadily along clear, straight portions of the planned path
while the camera continuously scans for obstacles. If a detected obstacle
blocks the route, Nav2 updates the costmaps and replans around it. A valid path
is retained rather than replaced every second. The controller also makes small
heading corrections, slows near obstacles, and turns for corners already in
the map. Reliable passage through the three-cone zigzag remains unfinished.
